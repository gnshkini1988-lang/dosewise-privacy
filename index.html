Dosewise Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 November 2026 · Developer: Ganesh Kini · Contact: gnshkini1988@gmail.com

In short: Dosewise has no account, no ads and no servers of ours. The medicines and times you enter stay on your phone unless you choose to share your schedule with someone you trust (an optional feature, described below). Google's text-reading tool (ML Kit) also sends technical diagnostics to Google, described below.

What Dosewise stores on your phone

  • The medicines you add: name, strength, amount per dose and form (for example pill or capsule).
  • Your reminder times and the days of the week for each medicine.
  • Today's check marks (which doses you ticked off). These are deleted automatically the next day; Dosewise keeps no history.
  • Optionally, the name you want to be greeted with.
  • Optionally, the invites you have accepted from people you care for (a name and an invite code).

This is stored on your device. Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for this app, so it is not copied elsewhere, except when you turn on family sharing (below).

What we do not collect

Dosewise has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics of its own, and does not use your location. It does not read your contacts and does not request permission to read or send text messages.

Camera and label scanning

If you choose to scan a medicine label, Dosewise takes a photo, reads the text on it on your phone, and shows you possible medicine names to choose from. The photo is held in memory only while it is being read; Dosewise does not save it or upload it. You always confirm the name before anything is saved. Scanning is optional: you can type everything in instead.

Third-party service: Google ML Kit

Label reading uses Google's ML Kit text recognition library, which runs on your phone. According to Google, ML Kit may send technical diagnostics to Google over an encrypted connection, such as device model and operating-system version, the app's package name and version, and performance measurements. It is not given your medicine list, times or contacts. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure and Google's Privacy Policy.

Reminders, notifications and alarms

Dosewise uses Android notifications and alarms to remind you at the times you choose. It restarts these reminders after your phone restarts. These features work on your phone and do not send information anywhere.

Family sharing (optional)

This feature is off until you tap Create invite on the Profile screen. When it is on:

  • Your phone encrypts your medicine schedule (medicine names, doses, times, days), the name you entered, and which of today's doses you have ticked off. The encryption key exists only in your invite QR code and invite code; it is never uploaded.
  • Only the encrypted data is uploaded, to a Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) database that we operate. Because we do not have the key, we cannot read it. Anyone who has your invite code can read it, so share it only with people you trust.
  • To protect your upload, the app uses Firebase Anonymous Authentication, which gives your installation a random identifier. It is not linked to your name, email or phone number.
  • The upload is refreshed when your schedule changes, when you tick off a dose, and at your reminder times.
  • Google, as our service provider, processes this data; see Firebase privacy information.

If you accept someone else's invite, your phone downloads and decrypts their schedule to show it to you. It keeps only the invite code and the name on your phone.

Stopping: tap Stop sharing to delete the uploaded copy immediately. Erase all my data also deletes it. People you invited can no longer see your schedule after that. You can stop watching someone at any time from their screen.

Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete everything at any time with Settings → Erase all my data, which removes all medicines and reminders from your phone, stops any sharing and deletes the uploaded copy. Uninstalling the app also removes its data. You can delete a single medicine from its edit screen.

Children

Dosewise is intended for adults and for people who help look after them. It is not directed at children.

Not medical advice

Dosewise is a reminder tool. It does not give medical advice and cannot tell you what or when to take. Always follow your doctor's or pharmacist's instructions. If you miss a dose, ask them what to do.
